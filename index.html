Questions

Before you install

The things farmers usually ask us before they get started.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Logging poultry, eggs, feed, and expenses all work offline. Once you're back near a signal, everything syncs to your account automatically.

What does it cost?

Core flock tracking is free. A few advanced tools, including the dashboard, egg and incubation tracking, and the marketplace, are part of a paid subscription. Every first-time subscriber gets one month free before being charged.

Is my farm data private?

Your records belong to you. You can view, export, or permanently delete your data and account at any time from Settings. See our Terms and Privacy for the details.

What languages does it support?

English, Spanish, Swahili, French, Arabic, Hindi, and Chinese, with more on the way.

Can I manage more than one farm?

Yes. You can add a second farm to the same account and switch between them from the menu, each with its own flock, feed, and records.
